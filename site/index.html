Luciole Mono

Luciole, the typeface designed for visually impaired readers, now with every character the same width — for your terminal and your code.

Type your own text. 11 pt (15 px) is a particularly crisp size.

Characters that must not be confused

  • I l 1 |
  • 0 O o
  • ' ` "
  • ; : , .

The zero is slashed and narrower than the O; i, j, l and r are redrawn with serifs to fill their cell.

Install

  1. Download the four files above.
  2. On Linux, copy them to ~/.local/share/fonts/ and run fc-cache -f. On macOS or Windows, open each file and choose “Install”.
  3. Choose “Luciole Mono” in your terminal or editor settings, then restart it.

About

Luciole was designed for visually impaired readers by Laurent Bourcellier and Jonathan Perez, at the Centre Technique Régional pour la Déficience Visuelle (typographies.fr). Luciole Mono keeps its design and fits it to a 620-unit grid: wide letters are compressed then thickened back, narrow letters are redrawn, and the bold is a lighter semi-bold so that counters stay open.

Luciole Mono is a derivative of Luciole. It is an independent project, neither made nor endorsed by the authors of Luciole. Known limitations and the full list of changes are in the README.
